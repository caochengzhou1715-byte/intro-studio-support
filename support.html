How can we help?

Start with these quick checks. Your saved draft is kept if an export fails.

My preview won’t load

Return to Templates and reopen the template. If it requires a source video, import the original or a replacement in Media first.

My export didn’t finish

Keep the app in the foreground, check available storage and retry. If Photos permission is off, save to Files or share instead.

How do I delete my data?

Delete drafts and exported videos in Projects. Removing the app removes its local app data. Copies in Photos, Files, backups or shared elsewhere are managed separately. Back up work you want to keep first.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. This release lets you create and export without an account, subscription or in-app purchase.

Can I publish a video with the bundled music?

See the original track and CC BY 4.0 attribution in Music & Credits. Keep the required attribution with your published video.

Contact the developer

Report a problem or request help through the support tracker. Include your device, app version and the steps that led to the problem.

Open a support request

A GitHub account is needed to send a request. Requests and replies are public. Do not include passwords, email addresses, personal information or private media. You can check existing requests without an account.
